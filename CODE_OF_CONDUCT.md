# Code of conduct

SnapMark welcomes people who want to improve the software in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and respectful in technical discussion.
- Critique the change, not the person.
- Give enough context for others to reproduce a problem.
- Protect private information in screenshots, logs, and crash reports.
- Accept a maintainer's decision when a discussion has reached a clear conclusion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to [@perevernihata](https://github.com/perevernihata). The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed. Reports will be handled with as much confidentiality as the situation allows.
